# Undo and redo for plan changes

- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Status:** Approved
- **Approved by user:** yes — 2026-09-29

## 1. Problem / intent

Workspace-redesign Phase 03 gave place, move and remove an undo toast, but
it lasts 8 seconds. The user found that too short to read the toast (now
longer, with the knock-on warning) and then act on it. The toast now pauses
while hovered or focused (d6464b1), but undo still depends on catching a
transient toast.

The user asked for real undo and redo: Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z,
plus Undo/Redo buttons in the plan header next to the "Completed through"
dropdown, covering every edit so the shortcut never silently skips one.
The audience is ANU Advanced Computing students editing their own degree
plan.

## 2. Requirements

### 2.1 Functional requirements

**Undoable edits**

- **UR1.** Every edit to an editable plan is undoable and redoable:
  - place, move and remove
  - pin ("Counts toward", including back to Automatic)
  - a check answer (Met, Not met, or Not sure, which clears the answer)
  - a specialisation or capstone choice
  - "Completed through" (the cutoff)
- **UR2.** An edit that changes nothing records no step and shows no toast.
  Examples: a course dropped on its own semester (already true through
  `changesNothing`), or the same pin, answer, choice or cutoff chosen
  again.

**History**

- **UR3.** There is one linear history per page visit, held in memory. It
  is cleared on reload or leaving the plan page. Changing the open course
  (`?course=`) does not clear it.
- **UR4.** Undo applies the newest step's inverse and moves that step to
  the redo side. Redo re-applies the newest undone step. Any new edit
  clears the redo side.
- **UR5.** The history keeps the latest 50 steps; older ones drop off.
- **UR6.** Each kind's inverse:
  - Move: back to its previous term.
  - Place: remove it.
  - Remove: place it back in its term, then restore its pin if it had one.
    Its check answers survive removal on the server, so they come back
    with it.
  - Pin, check answer, choice and cutoff: set back to the previous value.
  - Redo re-applies the original edit.
- **UR7.** If an undo or redo is refused or fails, the reason is announced
  and the step stays where it was (not moved to the other side).

**One path**

- **UR8.** All seven kinds go through Planner's `runAction`. Components
  stop calling `setPin`, `setCheck`, `setChoice` or `setCutoff` directly,
  as they already did for `placeCourse` and `removeCourse` in Phase 03.
- **UR9.** One plan change is in flight at a time. While one is pending,
  Undo and Redo are disabled and their shortcuts do nothing.

**Toast**

- **UR10.** Every edit, undo and redo shows the toast. There is only ever
  one toast: a new edit rewrites it in place and restarts its 8-second
  timeout. The timeout still pauses while the toast is hovered or has
  focus.
- **UR11.** The toast reads `<message>.<knock-on>` (the knock-on as in
  WR47, for every kind). Messages:
  - Move: "Moved COMP2100 to S1 2029"
  - Place: "Placed COMP4680 in S1 2029"
  - Remove: "Removed COMP2100 — Software Construction"
  - Pin: "COMP2100 now counts toward Electives", or "COMP2100 now counts
    automatically" for Automatic
  - Check answer: "COMP2100: '<check label>' marked Met", "… Not met" or
    "… Not sure"
  - Choice: "Chose Artificial Intelligence for Specialisation"
  - Cutoff: "Completed through S2 2027", or "Nothing completed yet" at 0
- **UR12.** After an edit the toast offers **Undo**. After an undo it reads
  "Undid: <message>." plus any knock-on the undo itself caused, and offers
  **Redo**. After a redo it shows the original message and offers Undo.
  This supersedes the Phase 03 rule that undoing shows no toast.

**Header buttons**

- **UR13.** Undo (↶) and Redo (↷) icon buttons sit next to the "Completed
  through" dropdown in the plan header.
  - Each is at least 44px.
  - The accessible name and tooltip say what it will do, e.g. "Undo: Moved
    COMP2100 to S1 2029"; with nothing to do, just "Undo" or "Redo".
  - Each is disabled when there is nothing to undo or redo.
- **UR14.** The read-only example plan has no Undo/Redo buttons, and the
  shortcuts do nothing there.

**Shortcuts**

- **UR15.** Shortcuts:
  - Undo: Cmd+Z on macOS, Ctrl+Z elsewhere.
  - Redo: Cmd+Shift+Z on macOS; Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y elsewhere.
  - They act anywhere on the plan page, including inside the details
    panel, and prevent the browser default when they do.
- **UR16.** The shortcuts are ignored while focus is in a text `input`,
  `textarea`, `select` or `contenteditable` element, so the search box
  keeps native text undo. Once Phase 04's ⌘K palette exists, they are also
  ignored while it is open.

### 2.2 Non-functional requirements

- axe is clean with the buttons in their enabled and disabled states, and
  with the toast in its Undo and Redo forms.
- The header fits at 390×844 and 1920×1080 with no horizontal overflow.
  The card-height budget and fitted-page checks still hold.
- Controls are at least 44px (project rule).
- `pnpm check` is green on every commit (CLAUDE.md).

### 2.3 Out of scope

- History that survives a reload, or is shared across tabs or devices.
  Edits made elsewhere aren't tracked: undo applies its inverse to whatever
  the plan is now.
- Merging runs of edits (e.g. several check answers) into one step. It was
  considered and rejected as arbitrary.
- Undoing plan creation, "Copy plan link", or UI state: panel widths,
  collapsed sections, the open course, the prerequisite-links toggle.
- A visible history list or menu.

### 2.4 Assumptions (confirmed)

- Each non-course edit sets a single value with a known inverse. Confirmed
  in `src/lib/repo.ts`: `setPin(code, groupId|null)`, `setCheck(code, item,
  answer|null)`, `setChoice(groupId, childId|null)` and `setCutoff(n)`.
- Removing a course deletes only its `plan_courses` row
  (`repo.ts` `deletePlacement`), so its `plan_checks` answers survive and
  return when an undo re-places it.
- A move keeps the course's pin (`upsertPlacement` updates only the term),
  so undoing a move is a move back (as in Phase 03).
- Check answers are `"met" | "not-met"`, and absent means "Not sure"
  (`types.ts` `CheckAnswer`). The answer key is the check's `item` text,
  and its display text is `VerifyCheck.label`.

## 4. Design

**Actions.** `PlanAction` gains four kinds:

```ts
| { kind: "pin"; code: string; groupId: string | null }
| { kind: "check"; code: string; item: string; answer: CheckAnswer | null }
| { kind: "choice"; groupId: string; childId: string | null }
| { kind: "cutoff"; cutoff: number }
```

Every kind has an inverse built from the view before the change, the same
way `undoEntry` works today. Remove's inverse is a place followed by a pin
(the existing `restorePin`), or a small compound; the exact shape is left
to planning. `changesNothing` extends to the new kinds.

**History.** Planner holds `past: Step[]` and `future: Step[]` (at most 50
in `past`), where a step carries its action, inverse and message. Undo,
Redo, the toast's button and the shortcuts all call the same two
functions. Only one request is in flight at a time.

**Feedback.** One toast (the existing element) shows the latest of edit,
undo or redo, with Undo or Redo and the knock-on warning. The header
buttons name the next undo or redo.

**Alternatives considered:**
- Toasting only big changes: rejected as inconsistent.
- Toasting only when the result lands away from the control used: rejected
  as inconsistent.
- Merging runs of check answers into one step: rejected as arbitrary.
- A persistent header status line instead of a toast: rejected, to keep
  the toast.
- History surviving a reload: rejected, because of the staleness rules it
  would need.

## 6. Handoff notes for planning

- **Sequencing is open.** This touches the plan header, which Phase 04
  (search button) and Phase 05 (restyle) also change. Planning decides
  whether it runs as its own plan before Phase 04, and amends those phase
  files so the buttons keep their slot next to "Completed through".
- **Not to re-litigate:**
  - every edit is undoable
  - history lasts for the page visit
  - every edit toasts (one toast, rewritten)
  - no merging
  - icon buttons with named tooltips
  - undo shows "Undid: …" with Redo
- The existing specs for the undo toast (`spec/layout.test.ts`: "undo for
  every plan change", "knock-on warning", the toast placement suites, and
  the ":982"/":2961" "Removed COMP1130" assertions) must stay green or be
  updated for the new wording, never weakened.
- **Risk:** routing `setCheck` through `runAction` must keep the details
  panel's pending-answer display (`pendingCheck`), which stops the pressed
  button snapping back before the new view arrives.
